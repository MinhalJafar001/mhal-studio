The fix brings order, with the calendar, job card, quote and invoice building themselves on screen. The result slows down as the week clears and free time appears, and the film closes on a clear call to action to start a free trial. Every app screen was rebuilt in code so it animates crisply, and the phones tilt in 3D against floating objects for depth."
